A visible path from commitment to completion.

Each challenge carries its scope, funding, contributor handoff, delivery evidence, and review outcome in one shared record.

01 / Publish

Define and fund the work

Maintainers publish clear rules and reserve contributor rewards before intake opens.

02 / Accept

Reserve a challenge

Contributors accept an available slot and establish an accountable delivery record.

03 / Submit

Attach proof of delivery

Issue, pull request, and completion notes stay connected for review and dispute evidence.

04 / Verify

Review, rate, and release

Approval transfers the reserved reward; unused escrow remains accountable and refundable.

Contributor proof

Trust the work trail, not the pitch.

Delivery evidence, mutual ratings, and review decisions stay bound to the challenge so both sides can see how work reached its outcome.
