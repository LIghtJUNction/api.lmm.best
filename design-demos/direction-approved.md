## Production Constraints

- Use the Anthropic-art palette and editorial form grammar documented in `design-demos/brand-spec.md`.
- Keep the public and pre-activation surfaces truthful to the real bounty workflow.
- Do not expose provider, model, relay, gateway, or protocol terminology before activation.
- Preserve the existing wallet and payment conversion path.
- Preserve required license, NOTICE, and upstream attribution even while replacing public legacy branding.
